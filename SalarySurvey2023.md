# Salary Survey 2023
Intro:

We asked you guys anonymously via the Data Operations IL LinkedIn group in January/February 2023 to share how much you’re earning and for what type of job.

There were many challenges - we don’t have a clear title in most companies, so we used job responsibilities as an indicator. Also, we only had 43 answers to work with, so be sure to take anything presented here with a grain of salt.

So without further ado, with beginning of 2023 data, here are the results.
