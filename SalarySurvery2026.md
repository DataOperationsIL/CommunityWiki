# Salary Survey 2026


## Some important notes before you dive in:

- All salary numbers/rangers are global, unless otherwise noted (we had exactly 3 respondents with an hourly salary, they are noted separately). 
- All salary numbers are the Bruto numbers, not including extra benefits such as RSU’s, 10bis, etc.
- **Everything Must Be Taken With a Grain of Salt**. We only have 37 responses to the survey, so we opted to remove lines that represent exactly 1 person, and in many cases, you’ll see a comment “<3 respondents” which means we only have 2 answers in this line, so numbers are not reliable in any way.
